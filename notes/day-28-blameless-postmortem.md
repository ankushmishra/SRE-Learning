## Learning goals
- Describe an incident and its impact without assigning personal blame.
- Turn evidence into specific, verifiable follow-up actions.

## Fundamentals
A postmortem records what happened, impact, timeline, detection, contributing conditions, response, and improvements. Blameless analysis asks how tools, process, and context shaped decisions. Separate confirmed facts from unknowns; actions should have an owner placeholder, due date, and a way to verify completion.

## Practical session
1. Use your Day 26 or Day 27 local drill; do not invent an external incident.
2. Write `~/sre-followon-lab/notes/day28-postmortem.md` with summary, impact, timeline, detection, contributing conditions, response, and what went well.
3. Add two concrete improvement actions, each with an owner placeholder, due date, and verification method.
4. Mark unsupported causes as unknown and remove blame-oriented wording.

## Check your understanding
- Does each causal statement have evidence?
- Could another learner verify each action is complete?
- Does the note explain system conditions rather than faulting a person?

## Done when
The postmortem is evidence-based and has two trackable improvement actions.
